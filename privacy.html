Privacy Policy — Financial Dashboard Effective date: 2026-09-12 Financial Dashboard is a personal application operated solely by its owner and is not offered to other users. The application uses Enable Banking to obtain read-only access to bank accounts explicitly authorised by the owner. It may process account identifiers and booked transaction information, including dates, descriptions, currencies, and amounts. Imported financial information is stored in CSV files selected by the owner. Authentication keys are stored in the Apple Keychain on the owner’s device. The application does not operate an external database, display advertising, use analytics, sell personal information, or initiate payments. Bank information passes through Enable Banking and the connected banks as required to provide account-information services. Their own privacy notices also apply. Information remains stored until the owner edits or deletes the CSV files, resets the application, or removes the application. Bank access can be revoked through Financial Dashboard, Enable Banking, or the connected bank. This application is intended exclusively for the owner’s personal and non-commercial use.
